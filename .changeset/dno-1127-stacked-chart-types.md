Explore and widgets can draw a **Stacked bar** and a **Stacked area** chart, which show what made up a total rather than how each series moved. A stack needs a Group by; without one it is drawn as the plain bar or area it is and says so. When a breakdown has more series than fit, the smallest are stacked as one **Other** band so the height is still the real total, and the tooltip gives each bucket's total.
