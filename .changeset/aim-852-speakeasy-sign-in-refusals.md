When Speakeasy is the company sign-in provider and won't vouch for an account (for example, the person isn't a member, was deprovisioned, or used a sign-in method the organization doesn't allow), the connect page now tells them to ask their administrator or use a separate sign-in, and connection health records it as something an admin has to fix. Company sign-ins that need renewing are now recorded on MCP requests, too.
