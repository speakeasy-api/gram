When Speakeasy is the company sign-in provider and won't vouch for an account (for example, the person isn't a member, was deprovisioned, or used a sign-in method the organization doesn't allow), people are told to contact their administrator instead of being asked to reconnect again and again. These refusals now show up in connection health and connection log exports, naming the administrator as the one to fix them.
