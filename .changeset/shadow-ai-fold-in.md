Shadow AI folds into Security and Policy behind the `gram-shadow-ai-guardrails` flag: a Shadow AI policy in Guardrails with an Access, Scope and Review flow over the organization's whole scan target library, a Shadow AI targets tab, a Watchdog tile and a Risk Events category filter. `listAiScanTargets` now returns each target's access summary, and the Platform MCP's `list_ai_scan_library` reports each target's state, decision and rationale.
