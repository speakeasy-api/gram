Connection Logs now record why Enterprise Managed Authorization failed for a person: on a tool call to a server or a gateway member, and on the consent page, once per attempt however often it reloads. A gateway's consent page records it under each member it affects. Each row names the outcome: the identity provider refused (`access_denied`, `scope_policy_denied`, `invalid_target`), the service refused (`ema_provider_rejected`), the setup isn't right (`configuration_required`), permissions are missing (`insufficient_scope`), an answer couldn't be read (`malformed_response`, `malformed_assertion`), or a saved sign-in must be renewed (`reauthentication_required`). These refusals are no longer recorded as `invalid_remote_session`, and they are recorded even when a failed refresh of an older connection was recorded first, or when a gateway member's saved connection failed too; an outage is still the answer, recorded once. The app's refresh after a refusal now records who refused: `upstream_ema_idp_refused`, `upstream_ema_service_refused`, or `upstream_ema_not_renewed` (no one refused, such as a setup problem). `upstream_ema_refused` is no longer written and names no party, since older rows use it for any refusal. The server's status does not change.
