Connection Logs now record why Enterprise Managed Authorization failed for a person: on a tool call to a server or a gateway member, and on the consent page, once per attempt. A gateway's consent page records it under each member. The code names the outcome: the company sign-in service refused (`access_denied`, `scope_policy_denied`, blocked), the service refused (`ema_provider_rejected`), a setup problem (`configuration_required`, `invalid_target`), missing permissions (`insufficient_scope`), an unreadable answer (`malformed_response`, `malformed_assertion`), or a sign-in to renew (`reauthentication_required`). The row's detail names the failed stage (`exchange`, `validation`, `redemption`), so `insufficient_scope` and `malformed_response` show who sent them. Rows carry the service client and its provider. They are no longer recorded as `invalid_remote_session`, and are kept beside a failed refresh or a gateway member's failed saved connection. The app's refresh after a refusal records who refused: `upstream_ema_idp_refused` (blocked, like `access_denied`), `upstream_ema_service_refused`, or `upstream_ema_not_renewed` (no one refused). `upstream_ema_refused` is no longer written.
