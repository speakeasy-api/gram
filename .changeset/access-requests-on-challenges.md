Authorization Challenges now shows when a denied member has asked for that access. The row says when they asked and quotes their note, and its Review button opens the same decision as the Access Requests tab: approve for 1, 2 or 4 hours or until end of day, assign a role, or deny. Deciding a request also refreshes the challenge list, so denials resolved by an approval no longer stay on screen until reload.
