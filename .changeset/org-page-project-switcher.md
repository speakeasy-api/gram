Pages you don't have permission to view now keep the bar at the top of the page, so the project switcher is always there. On organization pages that switcher now opens a list of the projects you can open, as it does inside a project. Members whose access covers only some projects can get from an organization page they can't view into one of their projects. With no projects, the list says to ask an admin to add you to one. Members of several organizations can switch organization from the same menu.
