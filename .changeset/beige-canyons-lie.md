Offer the guided Okta setup flow only to an organization that is ready for it. Okta keeps its place in the provider grid either way; what the readiness checks decide is whether it carries the Guided badge and whether picking it branches into the advanced flow. Where it does not, Okta takes the same portal path as every other provider, under one sentence saying guided setup is not available for this organization yet. An organization that already has a live Okta connection keeps the advanced flow regardless, and until the check answers the card claims neither, so nothing is offered and then withdrawn.

Speakeasy staff see why. A readiness panel on the identity provider setup card, on the organization identity settings page, and on the organization record in the admin dashboard gives one row per check with a pass or fail mark, the detail, the remedy and who has to act on it, over the verdict and when it was last checked, with a Re-check action. It reports; it changes nothing, and none of it is shown to the customer.
