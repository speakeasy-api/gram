Shadow AI has its own page in the sidebar, next to Guardrails. It has two tabs: **MCP servers**, the MCP server inventory and its approvals, and **AI tools**, the library of AI tools that device agents look for. The page header opens the project's Shadow AI policy, or sets one up when there is none. Guardrails no longer has the MCP approvals and Shadow AI targets tabs; links to them open the matching Shadow AI tab. Command K finds the Shadow AI page and each Shadow MCP server by name.
