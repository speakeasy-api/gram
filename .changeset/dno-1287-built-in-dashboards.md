The Dashboards page lists the dashboards Speakeasy ships with the product, marked **Speakeasy-built**, beside the project's own. One opens read only at `/dashboards/builtin/<slug>` under the shared filter bar, with every card answering within it, and **Duplicate** makes a project dashboard from it, with a saved widget per card, that can be laid out and changed like any other. The first is **MCP & Tools**: what the agents called, how often a call failed, and who was calling.
