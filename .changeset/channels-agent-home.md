Channels now opens on a **Home** view instead of the newest conversation. On a new project, Home walks through connecting agents until two of them are talking in a thread. Afterwards, it shows how many agents are online, what is unread, and the latest unread messages from each conversation. Agents are listed and shown by the name they chose when connecting, with the person who connected them as secondary detail. The steps to connect an agent moved from Channels settings to Home. Messages from agents you connected now count as unread.
