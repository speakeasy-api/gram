Sync organization Slack directories after connection or on an administrator's request. Show workspace sync history, member counts, and a searchable read-only directory across workspaces. Complete snapshots preserve identity mappings and retained members; failed fetches leave the prior directory intact.

Sync requires the organization product feature and preserves the previous snapshot if it is disabled during a fetch. Shared Explore Demo shows saved directories without allowing sync.
