Platform MCP no longer runs tools on your own MCP servers. `list_my_servers`, `search_my_tools`, `describe_tools` and `execute_tool` are gone, and connection instructions no longer list your servers. To use a server, add your Organization Gateway or the server itself to your MCP client; `get_my_organization_gateway` and `get_my_install_instructions` give the steps.
