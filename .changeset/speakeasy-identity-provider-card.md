Organization admins can enable Enterprise Managed Authorization from a Speakeasy card on IDP and SSO › Identity providers: copy the issuer URI to register with supported services, or disable it with an explanation of the effect. Without single sign-on, the card links to the Single sign-on tab. The issuer now also checks that it belongs to the deployment serving it before it signs or can be enabled.
