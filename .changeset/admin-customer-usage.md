Staff can see every paying customer's estimated usage on one admin page. Customer usage, under Platform management, shows each customer's estimated cost over time in a grid of cards, bucketed by that customer's own billing cycle, with this cycle's spend, the change against the same point last cycle, and the product breakdown. Search, plan filter, sort, product selection, interval, and the cumulative toggle apply to every card and are kept in the URL.
