Enterprise Managed Authorization is now its own section on a remote MCP server's settings. It reads as a chain: the sign-in, who vouches, what the service trusts, and the remote session client, each linking to where it is changed, with one Turn on or Turn off action. The Identity providers tab gains a Connected services table and a page per service with what the service trusts, the identity providers it accepts (one checkbox each), and the affected servers. The Speakeasy provider page lists the services that use Speakeasy as their identity provider.
