Enterprise Managed Authorization is now its own section on a remote MCP server's settings. It reads as a chain: the sign-in, who vouches, what the service trusts, and the remote session client, each linking to where it is changed, with one Turn on or Turn off action. The Identity providers tab gains a Connected services table and a page per service with what the service trusts, the identity providers enabled for it (one checkbox each), and the servers that connect to it. The Speakeasy provider page lists the services that trust Speakeasy. When the server connection's scopes change after Enterprise Managed Authorization is on, the section says so and offers Apply connection scopes.
