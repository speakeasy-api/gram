Manage killswitches from the Access tab of the person they restrict, instead of a separate organization-wide Killswitch screen. Listing, creating, editing, lifting and the full version history of a killswitch all happen on that person's page; team, connection and session entry points open it. The killswitch mark beside a person's name is now an icon whose hover text says what the state stops. The retired Killswitch addresses redirect: the roster to Identities, and a single killswitch to its subject's Access tab with that record open, so audit-log links still land on the exact record.
