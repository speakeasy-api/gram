New self-serve free trials start after an organization admin adds a card. The card is saved with Stripe and is not charged during the 14-day trial. Prepaid cards are not accepted, and a card can start only one free trial. Until a card is added, the organization shows a **Start your free trial** page; members who are not admins are asked to contact an admin. When the trial ends, the saved card is offered when you start pay-as-you-go billing. Trials that are already running are not affected.
