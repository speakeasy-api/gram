Give each identity tab a widget suited to its question instead of the same list of rows. Tool usage carries its failure share on the bar, cost shows where the tokens went and how much came from cache, security shows the category split and rule concentration, access shows roles as chips and the allow/deny ratio, and activity gets a per-day column chart — the rhythm a row list cannot show. Lists are cut to a recent sample, since the handoff owns the long tail. The bare identity URL now redirects to the overview tab rather than rendering an empty pane.
