A dashboard has the shared filter bar above its cards, offering a date range and the fields (user, agent, model, MCP server, status) some card can be filtered by; every card answers within it. A dashboard opens on its saved filters, or on the past seven days, unless the link says otherwise. Picking in the bar is the viewer's own view until **Save filters** stores it as what the dashboard opens on for everyone; **Reset filters** returns to what is saved.
