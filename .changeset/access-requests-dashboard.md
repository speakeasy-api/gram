Roles & Permissions has an Access Requests tab listing members' pending requests. Reviewing one, or opening it from the email or Slack link, lets an administrator approve it for 1, 2 or 4 hours or until the end of the day, deny it, or assign a role. A member who has asked sees their request as pending on the page they were blocked on, and access that can't be given temporarily tells them to ask an administrator instead.
