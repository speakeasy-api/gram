Suspected problems where every person's app asked for a connection on a server that is missing, has expired, or can no longer be used now say "N people need to connect an account for <server>" instead of "can't sign in", and say that a connection link their app shows can fix it. Problems each person fixes themselves are now listed after the rest, and each problem can say who has to fix it (`who_fixes`). A seeded demo or local organization whose logs feature is newer than its seeded connection events has it moved back once, then left alone, so its seeded suspected problems are listed.
