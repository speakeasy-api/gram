Organization admins can import Gemini web chats into Agent Sessions through Google Vault. Connecting takes two saves: paste the service account key and enter a delegated Workspace admin, then pick the organizational unit to export, or Entire organization. Speakeasy finds or creates its own Vault matter, and syncs completed UTC days every hour. When Google refuses access, the error names the delegation scope or Vault privilege to fix.
