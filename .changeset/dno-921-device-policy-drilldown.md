The managed devices table on an MDM integration page now says whether each device with an active agent enforces its policy: enforcing, behind, not reporting, or paused by a blocked agent version. The coverage tiles break active devices down the same way. Clicking a device opens a panel with each tool's enforcement state and what is holding it back, the agent's versions, and a prominent notice when enforcement is paused. The identity page flags a device that is behind or paused.
