An organization admin who signs in to an organization that already exists from another Speakeasy product now starts the same 14-day enterprise trial as a new signup. Those organizations previously stayed behind the book-a-demo gate, because a trial was only armed when the account had no organization yet. A member cannot start the trial. An organization that already has a trial, is on a paid tier, or was created as a prospect still follows its existing path.
