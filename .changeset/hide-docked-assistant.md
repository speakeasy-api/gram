Replace the always-on docked "Ask anything" Project Assistant composer with controls in the project top bar. The top bar is now a light grey row with the Dashboard/Headless switch on the left and New chat, Recent chats and search on the right; New chat drops the expanded dock, with its suggestions, down from under the header, and Cmd+/ (Ctrl+/) starts a new full-page chat. The project switcher moves to the top of the sidebar. Chat pages have no top bar, the full-page chat composer stays at the bottom of the viewport, the /chat landing is vertically centered, and pages keep their header while their data loads.
