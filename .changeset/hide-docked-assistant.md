Replace the always-on docked "Ask anything" Project Assistant composer with a grey bottom bar on every project page. Its "New Chat" button opens the expanded dock, with its suggestions, just above the bar, and its history button lists recent chats in a popover. Cmd+/ (Ctrl+/) starts a new full-page chat. Pages can no longer hide the assistant entry point, and the full-page chat composer now stays at the bottom of the viewport.
