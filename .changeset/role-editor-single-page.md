The role editor now shows a role's permissions on one page instead of two tabs. **MCP access** is a grid of projects and servers by Connect, View and Manage, with an **All tools** checkbox per row; click a server to unfold its tools and tick them one by one. **Platform access** is a card per area with one level each. Blocking moved out of the grid: **Deny explicitly…** is the last item of a row's, tool's or card's menu, asks for a confirmation, and every deny a role holds is listed in one **Explicit denies** box and counted beside Save. A new role can start from a template, and an existing role has a **Reset** button that puts it back as saved.
